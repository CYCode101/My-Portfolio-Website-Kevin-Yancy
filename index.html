A little about me

Curious about technology.
Focused on what comes next.

I am a dedicated first-year AI Engineering student at Mapúa University Intramuros, beginning my journey in a field that continues to shape how people learn, work, and solve problems. I enjoy exploring emerging technologies and building a strong foundation in the ideas behind them—from logical thinking and computing concepts to the ways intelligent systems can be designed responsibly.

Why I chose AI Engineering

Artificial intelligence fascinates me because it brings together analytical thinking, creativity, and practical problem-solving. I chose AI Engineering to better understand how intelligent systems learn from information, recognize patterns, and support decisions. My goal is to keep developing my skills, ask thoughtful questions about the technology I use, and contribute to a future where smart solutions are useful, responsible, and designed with people in mind.

15 Sep 2026AIE100 · Reflection 01

My First Weeks in AIE100

My first weeks in AIE100 have helped me see AI Engineering as more than writing code—it also involves understanding problems, thinking logically, and considering the people affected by a solution. I am learning to approach unfamiliar topics with patience and curiosity, knowing that strong fundamentals will make more advanced concepts easier to understand.

Learning mindset · Foundations
03 Oct 2026AIE100 · Reflection 02

Understanding the Impact of AI

Exploring AI's impact has reminded me that a technology can be powerful while still raising questions about fairness, privacy, and accountability. As I continue learning, I want to evaluate not only whether a system works, but also how responsibly it is designed and how its outcomes may affect different people.
